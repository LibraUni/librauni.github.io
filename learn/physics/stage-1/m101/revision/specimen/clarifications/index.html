Specimen marking clarifications

M101 specimen v0.1 · Addendum 1 · 24 October 2026

How to use this addendum

Read this with the specimen marking guide. These clarifications supersede the three restrictive passages identified below. The specimen questions, criterion identifiers, 100 marks, practice status and outcome allocation are unchanged. The original guide and calibration record are preserved.

Q7c · Trace the requested totals

Criterion Q7c.1 requires the first two totals, 1 and 1. A separate column listing visited x values is useful working but is not required by the prompt. Award this mark for the correct first two totals without that column. The third total, printed value, explanation of endpoint weighting and corrected expression retain their separate marks.

For example, “total is 1, then 1, then 2; the printed value is 2” earns criteria 1–3. It does not supply the explanation or correction needed for criteria 4–5.

Q2b · Follow-through works in either elimination order

Award the valid elimination criterion independently of which variable is found first. After one local arithmetic slip in that first variable, a consistent recovery of the second variable may earn its result criterion by follow-through: criterion 2 for x when y was found first, or criterion 3 for y when x was found first. The incorrect first variable loses its own result mark. Annotate the original slip and the follow-through award. A false verification earns no checking mark.

For example, correct elimination gives 3y=3. If the student then writes y=2 and consistently obtains x=3 from x−y=1, award criterion 1 and criterion 2 by follow-through: 2/4 without a valid check. The reverse order receives the same treatment: from 3x=6, an arithmetic slip x=3 followed by y=2 earns criterion 1 and criterion 3 by follow-through, also 2/4 without a valid check.

Q5b · Equivalent substitutions

The worked substitution is one valid route. Accept u=x² with du=2x dx, transformed bounds 0 and 1, integrand 1/(1+u), antiderivative ln(1+u) and definite value ln 2. These supply criteria 1–6 respectively. Any other valid substitution receives the corresponding evidence-based credit. The request to show transformed bounds and the definite integral in the new variable still applies.

Calibration and answer length

Review of the six existing constructed responses against these clarifications leaves their scores unchanged: 39, 40, 41, 40, 40 and 40. Their original evidence already satisfies the clarified criteria wherever those marks were awarded. These are constructed cases, not learner results.

A brief reason or a few lines of working is sufficient unless more is requested. The full worked solutions explain the mathematics; their length is not a required answer length. All requested checks and reasons still count.
