Before you begin

This specimen brings together the four blocks of M101. It is an opportunity to practise selecting a method, making its assumptions visible and deciding what the result establishes. Attempt it before opening the marking guide. After the sitting, use the guide to distinguish a lost calculation mark from a missing idea, then retry the parts that exposed a difficulty.

Practice conditions · 100 marks · 180 minutes

Answer all eight questions. Plan about 10 minutes to read, 150 minutes to work and 20 minutes to check. The suggested question times are working time only. Show intermediate reasoning, requested checks and relevant units. Exact answers are preferred where convenient; use radians and state rounding for approximations.

Use only this question paper, blank working paper, the unannotated M101-EXAM-REF-0.1 reference sheet and a basic scientific calculator. Permitted calculator functions are arithmetic, powers, roots, trigonometric functions, exponentials and logarithms. Disable or avoid symbolic algebra, graphing, matrix/system solvers, numerical differentiation/integration and stored notes. Do not run Python, browse, use generative assistance or consult other people or solutions during the attempt.

Write by hand or type your reasoning. Record your start and finish times and any interruption, resource departure or agreed accessibility adjustment. This is self-administered practice; it is not described as invigilated. Stop answering at 180 minutes. Up to 60 minutes afterwards may be used only to scan/export and organise the finished script; no submission service or deadline is activated by this practice paper.

Budget this sitting and three hours of review/correction within the existing 18-hour final-assessment allowance. They are not additional tasks charged to the separate 12-hour revision allowance. Take a break before reviewing. The complete marking guide contains answers; open it when ready to review.

Q1. Vectors and geometric meaning

12 marks · suggested working time 18 minutes

(a) [4 marks]

Points have position vectors a=(2,−1,3)m and b=(−1,3,3)m, relative to the same Cartesian frame. Find the displacement from A to B and its magnitude. Explain what the magnitude measures.

(b) [4 marks]

For dimensionless vectors u=(2,−1,2) and v=(−2,1,2), find their scalar product and the signed scalar projection of u in the direction of v. Explain the sign of the projection.

(c) [4 marks]

Let p=(1,2,0) and q=(0,1,2) be dimensionless vectors. Calculate p×q and the area of the parallelogram they span. Describe how interchanging p and q changes the result and the area.

Q2. Transformations and linear systems

12 marks · suggested working time 18 minutes

(a) [4 marks]

Let
S=(1101),R=(0−110),p=(2−1).
Apply S first and R second to p. Also calculate SRp, and use the two results to explain why the order matters.

(b) [4 marks]

Solve 2x+y=5, x−y=1 by elimination, and check the pair in both equations.

(c) [4 marks]

An equivalent reduced system is x+2y=3, λy=μ, where λ and μ are real parameters. State precisely when it has a unique solution, no solution, or infinitely many solutions. Give the solution family in the last case and justify each classification.

Q3. Limits and differentiation

14 marks · suggested working time 21 minutes

(a) [4 marks]

For f(x)=(x2−4)/(x−2), state the largest real domain, find limx→2f(x), and explain whether f is continuous at 2. State the value that would make its extension continuous there.

(b) [4 marks]

Starting from the difference quotient, derive the derivative of g(x)=1/x for x≠0. State the restrictions needed while simplifying.

(c) [6 marks]

Differentiate F(x)=x2e−x+ln(1+x). Identify the rules used and state the largest real domain of F and its derivative.

Q4. Using a derivative

12 marks · suggested working time 18 minutes

(a) [7 marks]

For f(x)=x3−3x on [−2,3], find and classify the interior stationary points. Determine the global maximum and minimum values and every point where each is attained. Explain why your checks cover all candidates.

(b) [5 marks]

Construct the tangent-line approximation to g(x)=4+x at x=0 and use it to estimate g(0.1). A proposal also uses this line to claim g(−3.9)≈1.025 with error below 0.01. Assess that claim using the domain, distance from the base point, and a calculator check.

Q5. Integration and the fundamental theorem

16 marks · suggested working time 24 minutes

(a) [4 marks]

Let A(x)=∫1xlntdt, for x>0. Find A(1) and A′(x), stating why the fundamental theorem applies. Is A increasing or decreasing on (0,1)? Justify your answer.

(b) [6 marks]

Evaluate ∫012x/(1+x2)dx by substitution. Show the transformed limits and the integral in the new variable.

(c) [6 marks]

Find ∫xcosxdx by integration by parts, and check the result by differentiation. Angles are in radians.

Q6. Signed energy transfer

12 marks · suggested working time 18 minutes

(a) [4 marks]

A device receives or releases energy at signed power P(t)=3t2−12t+9 watts, where t is the numerical time in seconds, 0≤t≤3. Positive power means energy enters; negative power means energy leaves. The net energy change equals the integral of signed power. Write the integral for the net change over the full interval, state its units and explain why it retains the sign of P.

(b) [4 marks]

Evaluate this net energy change and interpret its sign in the device model.

(c) [4 marks]

The total energy transferred, irrespective of direction, is ∫03|P(t)|dt. Find it, showing where you split the integral. Explain why it differs from the net change.

Q7. Numerical evidence and code interpretation

12 marks · suggested working time 18 minutes

(a) [4 marks]

For f(x)=x², forward differences at x=1 give 2.2 for h=0.2 and 2.1 for h=0.1. At h=10⁻¹⁶ a particular rounded calculation stores f(1+h)=f(1)=1 and returns zero. Use the analytic derivative to identify the better of the first two estimates. Explain their error and why the smallest step fails.

(b) [3 marks]

For f(x)=(x−1)² on [0,2], the values at 0,1,2 are 1,0,1. Calculate the two-panel trapezoidal estimate. A one-panel estimate is 2 and an independently derived analytic integral is 2/3. Quantify the error in your estimate and state what this comparison establishes about the claim that refinement alone proves exactness.

(c) [5 marks]

Hand-trace this attempted implementation of the two-panel trapezoidal calculation. Give total after each loop iteration and the printed value. Explain the error in the endpoint weights and write a corrected expression for this three-value calculation. Do not run code.
h = 1.0
total = 0.0
for i in range(3):
    x = i * h
    total += h * (x - 1)**2
print(total)

Q8. A detector calibration decision

10 marks · suggested working time 15 minutes

(a) [4 marks]

A detector records a spot at Cartesian position r=(3,4)Tmm relative to its origin. A software calibration maps positions by S=(1002). Interpret the two original components and calculate the spot's original distance from the origin. Find Sr and decide, with a reason, whether S can be a rotation of the coordinate picture.

(b) [2 marks]

For the same detector, a separate rate model is q(t)=2+t expected counts per second on 0≤t≤2 seconds. State the mathematical operation needed to find (i) the instantaneous rate of change of q at t=1 and (ii) the expected accumulated count from 0 to 2. Give the relevant derivative or integral expression; evaluation is not required.

(c) [2 marks]

The original rate model gives expected count 6.00 on both a coarse and a refined trapezoidal grid. Multiplying q by a revised detector gain of 1.05 gives 6.30 on either grid. Distinguish the gain change from grid refinement and propose an independent mathematical check of the original count.

(d) [2 marks]

An independent measurement reports an expected count in the interval [6.20,6.60]. Using the supplied evidence, assess whether the revised-gain prediction is more consistent with this measurement, and whether the evidence proves the revised model correct.

End of paper. Check question labels, signs, units, endpoint comparisons and whether your conclusions follow from your working. Record any unanswered parts.
