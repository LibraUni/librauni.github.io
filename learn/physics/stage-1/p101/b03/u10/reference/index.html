P101 · Unit 10

Light, images and observational limits · Reference

Recall: retrieve fluently. Understand: explain reasoning and conditions. Consult: look up a pattern. Printable reference PDF.

UseTopicResult and conditions
RecallVacuum lightc≈3.00×108ms−1; c=fλ0, T=1/f. Spectrum, increasing frequency: radio → microwave → infrared → visible → ultraviolet → X-ray → gamma ray.
RecallReflectionθr=θ1. Angles from the local normal; rays and normal coplanar. Surface angle =90∘−θ1.
RecallRefractionn=c/v; n1sinθ1=n2sinθ2. Stationary boundary: frequency fixed, λ=λ0/n. Higher index: smaller speed and normal angle.
UnderstandCritical angleFor n1>n2: θc=sin−1(n2/n1). Above it: total internal reflection at an ideal transparent lossless interface. Inverse sine is not reciprocal sine.
RecallThin converging lens1/f=1/do+1/di; f>0,do>0. Real image beyond lens: di>0; virtual, object side: di<0. Thin, paraxial lens in air; common length units.
UnderstandImage geometryParallel ray → far focus; central ray → straight; near-focus ray → parallel. m=hi/ho=−di/do. Negative m: inverted. do=f: no finite image plane.
RecallIntensity and energyI=P/(4πr2) / W m⁻². Steady isotropic small source, r>0, negligible losses. Small perpendicular collector: Preceived=IA, E=IAΔt for steady intensity.
UnderstandDistance ratioUnchanged source/conditions: I2/I1=(r1/r2)2. Source power differs from received power. 1cm2=10−4m2.
UnderstandRecorded valuesMatched additive background: source estimate R−B. Counts depend on exposure and response settings. Linear unsaturated response needed for proportional comparisons.
UnderstandObservational limitsResolution: distinguish nearby features. Magnification: image/display size. Cadence: time between samples. Exposure: accumulation interval. Finer sampling cannot undo optical blur or exposure averaging.
ConsultValidationCheck an absolute value, ratio, units, zero-power case and invalid inputs. Preserve raw records, settings and provenance. Hand agreement checks implementation; measured evidence tests the physical model.
ConsultSampling comparisonCompare full and sparse records on common axes; check sampling phase. Nearly equal samples do not establish a steady source between them. Repeated/control measurements test specified alternatives.
