Momentum, work and energy balances

Printable reference PDF

Recall: retrieve · Understand: explain/derive · Consult: look up. Conditions belong with formulas.

Momentum and work

UseTopicResult and conditions
RecallMomentump=mv; Px=∑mivix. Units kg m/s. Common inertial frame; signed components. L1
RecallImpulseJx=F―xΔt=Δpx. Units N s = kg m/s. Same interval; constant mass for Δpx=m(vx−ux). L1
UnderstandSystem balancePfx−Pix=Jext,x. Fixed matter; internal impulses cancel. Negligible external impulse gives approximate conservation. L1
RecallStickingv=mAuA+mBuBmA+mB. Positive masses; common final velocity; negligible external impulse. All velocities signed. L2
UnderstandCollision checksMomentum alone cannot determine two unknown final velocities. Elastic: total K unchanged. Sticking generally reduces K; total energy is not destroyed. L2
RecallConstant-force workW=F·s=Fscosθ; W=Fxsx+Fysy. θ: angle between vectors. J = N m. Particle model; constant force vector. L3
RecallKinetic energyK=12mv2≥0; Wnet=Kf−Ki. Add all force works. v=2Kf/m gives speed, not direction. L3
UnderstandZero workZero displacement or perpendicular force gives zero work. Zero net work can also mean cancellation of nonzero contributions. L3

Energy and interpretation

UseTopicResult and conditions
RecallUniform gravityΔUg=mg(hf−hi)=−Wg; Ug=mgh for chosen zero. Heights vertical; constant g. Energy belongs to Earth–object interaction. L4
UnderstandMechanical energyEmech=K+Ug; ΔEmech=Wother. Do not count Wg again. Conserved only if other net work vanishes. L4
ConsultExplicit ledgerKf=Ki+mg(hi−hf)+Win−Ed. Ed≥0: dissipated amount. Do not also subtract the same friction work. Units J. L4
UnderstandFeasibilityKf<0 rejects the stated endpoint. Kf≥0 alone does not check barriers, contact or travel time. Numerical agreement is not experimental validation. L4
ConsultMethod choiceForce/acceleration: Newton. Force/time: impulse. Force/displacement: work. Collision then climb: separate intervals and assumptions. L5
