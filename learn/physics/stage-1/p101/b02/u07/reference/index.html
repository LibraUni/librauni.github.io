P101 · Unit 7

Thermal transfers and testing a motion model · Reference

Use after the teaching: Recall means retrieve fluently; Understand means explain conditions; Consult means look up a pattern. Download the printable PDF.

UseTopicResult and conditions
RecallTemperatureT/K=θ/∘C+273.15. A change of 1 °C equals a change of 1 K. Do not add the offset to a temperature interval.
UnderstandState and transferInternal energy belongs to the system’s microscopic state. Heat is transfer caused by a temperature difference; work is another transfer mechanism. Neither is stored “heat”. Choose the system boundary.
RecallFirst lawΔEint=Q+Won=Q−Wby when other stored-energy changes are negligible. Inward heat and work on the system are positive.
UnderstandHeat capacityΔEint=CΔT=mcΔT, with C in J/K and c in J/(kg K). Use an appropriate approximately constant capacity, fixed phase and sufficiently uniform temperature; include other energy changes when relevant.
RecallIsolated equilibriumTf=C1T1+C2T2C1+C2. Positive capacities; common final temperature; no external heat, work or phase change. Include a vessel as another term if needed. The endpoint lies between the starting levels.
UnderstandMicroscopic modelFor a dilute ideal monatomic gas, temperature tracks mean translational kinetic energy per particle. Total internal energy also depends on particle number. This is not a universal microscopic formula for all materials.
RecallRates of motionv=dxdt, a=dvdt. For x=x0+v0t+12at2, constant a gives v=v0+at. A zero of velocity is a turn only if velocity changes sign.
UnderstandSpeed and signsSpeed is |v|. Away from v=0, equal signs of v and a mean increasing speed; opposite signs mean decreasing speed. Displacement and distance are different.
ConsultCentred estimatev(t)≈x(t+h)−x(t−h)2h, with h>0 and both sample times inside the domain. Reducing h can reduce approximation error while amplifying rounded-input effects.
ConsultStopping modelsFor initial speed v>0, constant braking magnitude b>0: tA=vb, dA=v22b. A constant-speed delay τ≥0 adds τ to time and vτ to distance.
RecallResidualri=dobserved,i−dpredicted,i; MAR=1n∑i|ri|. Keep repeated observations. Plot residuals as well as calculating a summary.
UnderstandEvidence and units1 mph = 0.44704 m/s; 1 ft = 0.3048 m. A benchmark tests implementation. Observations test predictions under documented conditions. A smaller MAR alone does not identify a cause or validate a universal law.

Consult · Python comparison pattern

Here speed_mph and distance_ft are NumPy arrays, b is positive and tau is nonnegative. Check inputs and retain the full residual plot.

v = speed_mph * 0.44704
observed = distance_ft * 0.3048
predicted = v**2 / (2*b) + v*tau
residual = observed - predicted
mar = sum(abs(r) for r in residual) / len(residual)
