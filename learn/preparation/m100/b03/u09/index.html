U09-L01 · Exponential change and logarithms

Equal additive steps produce a line; equal multiplicative factors produce exponential change. In N=N₀a^t, increasing t by1 multiplies N by a. A power law y=kx^p behaves differently: multiplying x by a factor multiplies y by that factor raised to p. log_b(x) is the exponent giving x, with x>0,b>0,b≠1. Products become sums of logs, quotients differences, powers factors. Never take the logarithm of a dimensional quantity directly: form a positive ratio to a reference quantity.

U09-L02 · Choosing axes and reviewing a refactor

np.exp evaluates e to a power; np.log is natural logarithm. semilogy changes only the vertical scale; loglog changes both. Positive values are required on logarithmic axes. A straight-looking plot is not proof of a physical mechanism. Before accepting a suggested refactor, specify input meaning, domain, expected outputs and boundary tests. Reject a refactor that changes those properties even if its code is shorter.
