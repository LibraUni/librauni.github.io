Working independently

Notes, these lessons, a calculator and documentation for taught Python syntax are permitted. Write your own answers and code. Do not use generated solutions, agents or another person’s answers. Ask for clarification if wording is unclear; record any assistance that affects your work. Reading a page does not begin or submit an attempt.

Allow 60 minutes, already included in the corresponding unit checkpoint (U02, U07 or U11). Ten questions, 10 marks each, no negative marking. Complete all questions before Submit. Numeric answers accept ordinary decimals, e notation or one fraction a/b; use no units or % symbol in boxes. Fractions are compared numerically with a relative tolerance of one part in a million. Feedback appears after a confirmed submission. This is an open-book computer-marked activity, not a secure examination. Later attempts are retained separately, not substituted for the original.
