U03-L03-S01 · 84 MINUTES · BLUEPRINT

Factor first, then cancel

Reverse distributivity by extracting a common factor; distinguish factors from terms and reject cancellation across addition.

Practice / assignment
Mix abstract factoring with a supplied relation. Evidence: an equivalence chain with restrictions and a counterexample to a faulty cancellation.
Expected evidence
Worked reasoning with any correction retained and explained; no requirement to submit every practice item.
Feedback
Provide two worked examples with fading support, then four graduated prompts and one error-analysis item; explain the first invalid step. Adjust item count after timing review, within this allocation.
Visual support
Factor-group highlighting; restrictions remain visible alongside every step.
Handover
Carry the explained result and any unresolved difficulty into Equivalent expressions with restrictions.
