U04-L03-S01 · 84 MINUTES · BLUEPRINT

Two constraints for two unknowns

Introduce substitution and elimination with explicit algebraic operations; explain why each step preserves the system.

Practice / assignment
Abstract systems then two supplied measurement constraints; substitute both recovered values into both original equations.
Expected evidence
Worked reasoning with any correction retained and explained; no requirement to submit every practice item.
Feedback
Provide two worked examples with fading support, then four graduated prompts and one error-analysis item; explain the first invalid step. Adjust item count after timing review, within this allocation.
Visual support
Two labelled information constraints, not a graph requiring U05.
Handover
Carry the explained result and any unresolved difficulty into Choose a method and verify both relations.
