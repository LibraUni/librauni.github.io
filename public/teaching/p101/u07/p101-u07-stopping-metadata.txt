CSV reformatted by LibraUni23October2026: columns renamed speed_mph,distance_ft; added1-based row; all50 pairs/order unchanged. No fitted or synthetic observations.
SI conversions: mph*0.44704=m/s; ft*0.3048=m. No rounding applied in calculations.
Source does not specify uncertainty, calibration, vehicle/surface identity or stopping-start event; do not infer precision from integer formatting.
Illustrative models: immediate constant braking b=3m/s2; comparison adds delay0.50s at initial speed. Parameters selected after inspecting scale, not independently measured or formally fitted. Sensitivity trial b=2.5m/s2.
