# LibraUni — visual identity

23 September 2026. Name, balanced academic/contemporary character, warm light default and optional dark mode confirmed. The black-hole logo with subtly integrated L and U is approved and adopted on the study desk.

## Purpose and character

An independent learning environment with the clarity and care of excellent university teaching. Scholarly, open, precise. The identity should support attention, exploration and sustained reading. No invented accreditation, institutional heritage, rankings or academic seals.

Suggested editorial line: “A place for deeper understanding.” Use sparingly on introductory pages; lessons lead with their subject.

## Logo

The approved black-hole mark combines sweeping lensing arcs and an equatorial disc with an L above and a U below. The first impression is a black hole; the initials emerge on closer inspection. Retain the rounded L elbow and flowing U of the approved artwork. Avoid making either letter heavier, squarer or more literal. This is symbolic artwork, not a scientific diagram.

Use the symbol with the LibraUni wordmark on home pages and navigation. Use the symbol alone for browser icons and compact contexts. Keep the spelling and capitalisation LibraUni. Never stretch, rotate, add shadows, enclose in a fake seal, or overlay busy photography.

Clear space: at least one quarter of the symbol's height on all sides. Recommended symbol width: 76px or more in navigation; lockup width: 180px or more. At favicon sizes the silhouette is an identifier, and the embedded letters will not be readable. Ink on ivory and a light reversed treatment on midnight are the primary combinations.

The unchanged approved master is /brand/librauni-approved.png (1536 × 1024). The site uses transparent derivatives from that master for production display: /brand/librauni-symbol-transparent.png and /brand/librauni-lockup-transparent.png. Dark surfaces use a CSS light treatment. The browser icon is an SVG container embedding the transparent symbol, not a traced vector logo. The artwork's wordmark remains part of the original image; compact navigation pairs its symbol with live Georgia text. The old horizon SVGs are superseded and must not be reused. A faithful vector master remains a future production refinement for large-format print. Do not regenerate or redraw the approved geometry without reviewing the result.

Brass is decorative, not small text on a light background. Borders can be subtle for grouping, but functional input boundaries must meet contrast requirements. Never use colour alone to communicate errors, completion, mastery or graph series. Use explicit text, markers and line patterns. Use light mode on first visit; preserve explicit user preference. Theme preference is a convenience on the device, not a study record.

## Typography

Display and brand: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif. Body and UI: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif. Monospace: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace. This system uses installed fonts and does not redistribute font files or require third-party font requests. Rendering varies slightly across platforms.

Lesson body: 18px target (16px floor for smaller contexts), line height 1.65–1.75, ideally 60–75 characters per line. Headings use restrained negative tracking and normal weight; display 44–74px, lesson titles 36–44px, section titles 28–36px. Labels 11–12px with modest tracking, only for brief metadata; avoid paragraphs of uppercase. Keep primary instructions at normal readable sizes.

Use proper mathematical typesetting with accessible underlying notation for complex equations when implemented. Simple specimen formulas use live HTML, not text embedded in images. Always define symbols and units. Do not shrink equations to fit; provide wrapping or labelled horizontal scrolling.

## Layout and components

Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px. Main page max width 1500px; lesson prose remains narrower. Corners mostly square or 3–6px; shadows rare. Separate sections with space and hairlines.

Hierarchy: Degree > Stage > Module > Block > Unit > Lesson. Breadcrumbs above titles; lesson context in sidebar; local lesson sections within reach. On mobile collapse side navigation into a labelled, keyboard-accessible menu. No hover-only actions.

Lesson pattern: title and purpose; prerequisites and estimated effort; explanation; worked examples; figures or simulations; practice; summary and next step. These are layout conventions, not an approved curriculum. Keep the core lesson free of promotional distractions.

Worked examples: numbered, warm inset surface, explicit reasoning. Practice: outlined block with a short action prompt. Hints and worked answers: separate disclosure controls, never automatically open. Optional depth: clearly labelled. Tutor note: quiet marginal/inset note with text label. Warnings: icon plus plain-language explanation, not colour alone.

Completion: show counts and a labelled progress bar. Mastery: separate assessment evidence and status; do not imply a page view is understanding. Never fabricate percentages or streaks. Use genuine milestones for encouragement; avoid pressure-based gamification.

## Scientific graphics and imagery

Use original SVG plots and diagrams for crisp scaling. Axes and units must be labelled, quantities defined, and captions interpret the graphic. Give every informative image useful alternative text. For complex plots include a data table or longer description. Plot primary series in teal, secondary in ochre, additional series in independently checked accessible colours; distinguish with dashes and markers as well.

Diagrams use consistent line widths, arrowheads and typography. Distinguish observed data, fitted models and construction lines. Decorative geometry must never masquerade as a physical model. Space photography belongs in relevant openings and lessons; captions, source, rights and scientific meaning must be checked individually. Avoid generic atom clip art and ambient star fields behind reading text.

## Voice

Precise, adult, inviting. Prefer “Try this”, “What changes?”, “Here is why” and specific feedback. Explain unfamiliar terms. Distinguish a model's assumptions from its conclusions. Avoid exaggerated claims about the material, faux institutional credentials, and empty motivational slogans.

## Accessibility and interaction

Verify WCAG AA text contrast for each actual foreground/background pair (4.5:1 normal text; 3:1 large text). Essential controls and graphical objects need 3:1 contrast where applicable. Keep visible focus, semantic headings, skip links, keyboard-operable controls, reduced-motion support and readable zoom/reflow. Targets should be at least 24px, preferably 44px for main actions. Check both themes; do not infer compliance from the palette alone.

The specimen is a review artefact, not the full learning application. Its theme toggle and hint disclosures work; illustrative breadcrumbs show the proposed hierarchy without pretending unavailable courses exist. It does not read or write learner data.

## Applications

Study desk: compact wordmark, clear task hierarchy, subdued progress indicators. Lesson pages: spacious light reading surface and precise figures. Module covers: subject title and one relevant diagram, generous margins, restrained colour. Reference sheets: white print background, ink wordmark, compact equation hierarchy, visible version/date. Social/share cards: midnight field, ivory title, small brass symbol, no faux university credentials.
