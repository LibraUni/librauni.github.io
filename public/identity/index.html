LAYOUT SPECIMEN / MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATIONS

Making change visible

A graph makes a relationship visible. Its slope tells us how quickly one quantity changes as another increases.

THE IDEA TO TAKE AWAY

A steeper line means a larger change in the vertical quantity for the same horizontal change.

From a line to a physical meaning

Imagine an object moving at a constant speed. At 2 seconds its position is 4 metres; at 6 seconds its position is 12 metres. The change in position is 8 metres over an interval of 4 seconds.

v = Δx / Δt = 8 m / 4 s = 2 m/s

PAUSE & THINK / 01

Read beyond the plotted points.

At the same constant velocity, how far does the object travel in the next 3 seconds?

A hint, if you need one

Each second contributes another 2 metres. How many of those equal intervals are there?

Show the worked answer

Distance = velocity × time = 2 m/s × 3 s = 6 metres. This is the additional distance, rather than the final position.
