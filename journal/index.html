Learning journal

What changed, what the evidence shows, and what needs attention next.

Your tutor maintains this academic record after tutorials: topics covered, evidence of understanding, changes observed, difficulties, feedback and next steps. Entries should be informative and direct, without repeating the whole lesson. You do not need to complete forms or import files for routine logging.

Available full tutorial transcripts are archived separately from session notes. If the tutor cannot access a transcript or save online, that gap will be stated. Website setup and administrative conversations do not belong here.

Dates use the supplied event time, or the server-recorded time if none was supplied. Entries with no known time remain in unfiltered exports. Event times and source labels are reported information, not independently authenticated tutor judgements.

Add your own observation (optional)

Your tutor handles routine logging. Use this only if you want to contribute a reflection or correction yourself.

Entries are preserved. Add a correction referring to the earlier entry instead of rewriting history. Record evidence and uncertainty; never treat attendance or confidence alone as demonstrated understanding.

Unsaved drafts stay on this device; they are not backed up until saved online. Server timestamps record when entries were saved. Your supplied event time records when the learning happened.

Optional transcript recovery

This is a fallback for a transcript your tutor could not access, not a routine task for you.

Import a UTF-8 .txt or .md transcript (up to 2 million characters). Preserve speaker names and original timestamps in the text. Long transcripts are stored in numbered parts without truncation. If its date is unknown, leave event time blank; the import time will still be recorded.

Saved academic entries are included in recursive private database backups. Downloads require sign-in and an online load; keep downloaded copies for offline access. Downloads contain academic records only.
