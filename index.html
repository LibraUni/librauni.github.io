Can I start learning now?+

Yes. Opening teaching in Mathematics for physics I (M101) and Physics, space & scientific thinking (P101) is available. More material is being developed incrementally. The full three-stage programme is not yet available.

Browse available lessons →
Do I need to know advanced mathematics?+

No advanced university mathematics is assumed at the beginning. The programme builds those tools progressively. You will need appropriate school-level foundations; check the module prerequisites and use targeted revision where needed.

See the mathematics starting point →
Will I study black holes and string theory?+

Quantum physics and special relativity have planned core homes. General relativity is introduced in the cosmology option. Explicit black-hole coverage and a late conceptual introduction to string theory are planning priorities; their detailed scope is still being developed. Technical string theory would be a separate optional pathway, if developed.

Is this an accredited university degree?+

No. LibraUni is an independent learning programme designed at BSc (Hons) level. Its credits and any awards are internal, not accredited university credits or a recognised degree.
