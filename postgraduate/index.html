01 / CHOOSE A DIRECTION

Ten ways to go deeper.

Each course builds on undergraduate physics or equivalent demonstrated preparation. The outcomes below describe our intended scope, not teaching already available.

01In planning

Quantum Field Theory

How do quantum fields describe particles and their interactions?

Develop field quantisation, multiparticle states, perturbative scattering, elementary QED and introductory renormalisation.

Preparation & recommended order

Prior knowledge. Advanced quantum mechanics, special relativity, analytical mechanics and mathematical methods.

Your route. A recommended starting point for advanced particle physics and technical string theory.

02In planning

String Theory

What changes when fundamental objects are extended rather than pointlike?

Study relativistic string dynamics and quantisation, connecting the mathematical framework to its motivations, limitations and open questions.

Preparation & recommended order

Prior knowledge. QFT and general relativity with the relevant geometry; equivalent demonstrated preparation is welcome.

Your route. Take QFT and the relevant GR foundations first. Further worldsheet and conformal-field methods will be explicitly taught or assigned.

03In planning

Loop Quantum Gravity

Can spacetime geometry itself have a quantum description?

Explore canonical gravity, spin networks, quantum geometry and selected applications, distinguishing mathematical results from unresolved physical questions.

Preparation & recommended order

Prior knowledge. Quantum mechanics, technical GR, constrained Hamiltonian systems and the required group theory.

Your route. Take GR first, then the canonical-gravity and mathematical preparation. QFT is useful background; string theory is not a prerequisite.

04In planning

Advanced Condensed Matter Physics

How do many particles produce entirely new kinds of behaviour?

Connect many-body models to collective phases, superconductivity and topological matter, using calculations and observational evidence.

Preparation & recommended order

Prior knowledge. Quantum mechanics, statistical physics, solid-state foundations and computational methods.

Your route. P303 or equivalent preparation is a useful route. Advanced Statistical Physics helps with critical phenomena; a full relativistic QFT course is not required to begin.

05In planning

Cosmology & Astrophysics

What can the universe tell us about its contents and physical laws?

Follow advanced cosmology or relativistic astrophysics: dark matter and dark energy, the early universe, compact objects, gravitational waves and inference from observations.

Preparation & recommended order

Prior knowledge. Relevant undergraduate cosmology or astrophysics, data analysis and GR appropriate to the chosen branch.

Your route. Take GR before advanced spacetime calculations. QFT precedes technical quantum early-universe work; observational study need not wait for it.

06In planning

Quantum Information & Quantum Computing

What can we learn, communicate and compute using quantum systems?

Analyse entanglement, quantum channels, algorithms, noise and error correction, with explicit attention to resources and limitations.

Preparation & recommended order

Prior knowledge. Operator-based quantum mechanics, linear algebra and probability.

Your route. An independent entry route after the relevant undergraduate foundations. QFT and GR are not required to begin.

07In planning

Advanced Particle Physics & the Standard Model

How do we calculate and test the interactions of fundamental particles?

Develop quantitative gauge-theory tools and study electroweak physics, the Higgs mechanism, QCD, flavour, neutrinos and experimental tests.

Preparation & recommended order

Prior knowledge. QFT competence, undergraduate particle physics and the required mathematical preparation.

Your route. Take QFT first. Non-Abelian gauge methods extend the elementary QED covered there.

08In planning

General Relativity & Gravitational Physics

How does spacetime geometry govern motion and gravitation?

Develop differential geometry and Einstein-equation calculations, then study black-hole geometry, causal structure, gravitational radiation and tests.

Preparation & recommended order

Prior knowledge. Special relativity, variational mechanics and multivariable calculus.

Your route. Can be studied alongside QFT. Its foundational portions prepare you for LQG, strings and advanced cosmology; introductory A302 coverage alone does not guarantee readiness.

09In planning

Advanced Statistical Physics & Complex Systems

How do fluctuations and interactions create collective order?

Investigate response, phase transitions, scaling, renormalisation, stochastic dynamics and selected nonequilibrium systems.

Preparation & recommended order

Prior knowledge. Undergraduate statistical mechanics, probability, differential equations and numerical methods.

Your route. A useful route towards advanced condensed-matter topics. Each course will identify shared methods so neither requires the other in a circular sequence.

10In planning

Plasma Physics & Fusion

How can we understand and control a collective system of charged particles?

Study plasma waves, kinetic theory, stability, magnetic confinement and fusion conditions, with connections to astrophysical plasmas.

Preparation & recommended order

Prior knowledge. Electromagnetism, mechanics and relevant fluid and mathematical methods.

Your route. An independent route after its undergraduate preparation. Helpful before specialised plasma and accretion topics in astrophysics.

02 / CONNECT YOUR STUDIES

A route, not a single ladder.

You do not need to take every course. “Take first” recommends a route to the skills needed for the named technical work; equivalent preparation is welcome. Some destinations need only the relevant foundational portions of an earlier course.

  1. Particles and strings

    QFT → Advanced Particle Physics

    QFT + GR and geometry preparation → technical String Theory

    Particle Physics is a useful further direction, not a compulsory extra step before every string course.

  2. Quantum gravity

    GR → canonical-gravity and mathematical preparation → LQG

    QFT is strongly recommended background. Strings and LQG are alternative interests; neither must precede the other.

  3. Quantum matter

    Quantum + statistical + solid-state foundations → Advanced Condensed Matter

    Advanced Statistical Physics is helpful before advanced critical phenomena. The courses will teach or recognise the necessary shared methods.

  4. The universe and black holes

    Relevant GR foundations → technical Cosmology & Astrophysics

    QFT + GR + statistical foundations → curved-spacetime QFT and black-hole thermodynamics

    Observational cosmology can begin with its own cosmology and data prerequisites. Quantum early-universe calculations need QFT.

Independent starting points. Quantum Information, Advanced Statistical Physics and Plasma Physics can each begin after their relevant undergraduate preparation. QFT and GR can be studied in parallel once their separate prerequisites are met.

03 / SHARED ADVANCED STUDY

Where fields meet gravity.

In planning — teaching not yet available

Quantum Fields in Curved Spacetime & Black-hole Thermodynamics

Explore how quantum fields behave when spacetime is curved, leading towards the Unruh and Hawking effects, black-hole entropy and the limits of semiclassical reasoning.

Take first: relevant QFT, GR—including horizons and causal structure—and statistical physics. This is a separate shared advanced course, beyond the introductory QFT specialisation. String theory and LQG are not prerequisites.

General Relativity & Gravitational Physics is a full specialisation above. Its foundational geometry and gravity teaching also supports other routes; we will identify the portions you need without duplicating an entire course.
