RecallCycle and sizeFull cycle: same stage and direction. Amplitude A: largest displacement magnitude from equilibrium. Peak-to-peak: 2A. Offset y0: equilibrium position.
RecallTime and phasef=1/T; ω=2πf=2π/T. T in s, f in Hz, ω in rad/s. One cycle: 2π rad.
RecallSinusoidal modely=y0+x; x=Acos(ωt+ϕ). Initial phase ϕ in rad. Positive maximum at tp: ϕ=−ωtp modulo 2π.
RecallSupplied SHM ratesv=−Aωsin(ωt+ϕ); a=−ω2x. Maximum speed Aω; maximum acceleration magnitude Aω2.
UnderstandSpring modelF=−kx, ω=k/m, T=2πm/k. Linear spring, fixed support, negligible damping and spring mass; k>0,m>0.
UnderstandMechanical energyE=12mv2+12kx2=12kA2 for the horizontal undamped model. |v|=(k/m)(A2−x2); direction needs cycle stage.
UnderstandDamping and drivingFree damping removes oscillatory mechanical energy. Steady driving balances loss per cycle. Lightly damped displacement resonance peaks near the undamped natural frequency.
RecallSample gridΔt=1/fs, tn=n/fs. Samples per cycle: fs/f. N intervals have N+1 endpoints. Record actual duration.
ConsultUpward crossingFor xi≤0<xi+1: tc=ti+−xixi+1−xi(ti+1−ti). Subtract equilibrium offset first; linear interpolation.
ConsultPeriod estimateT^=(cq−1−c0)/(q−1), q≥2 upward crossings. Inspect individual intervals; too few crossings means no estimate.
UnderstandAliasingDistinct motions can share samples. Use fs>2fmax with a justified signal-frequency bound; equality is unsafe. Graph/crossing estimates need resolution and duration checks.
