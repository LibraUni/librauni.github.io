P101 · Block 4

Fields, circuits and microscopic evidence

A force diagram describes the interactions on one chosen object. A field lets us ask a wider question: what would happen if we placed that object somewhere else?

In mechanics we followed bodies, calculated changes of momentum and balanced energy. In the wave block we examined patterns that travel and the observations they make possible. We now extend that reasoning to electric and magnetic interactions, to the movement of charge in circuits and to the evidence that matter has an atomic structure. The central habit remains the same: identify a physical system, choose a useful model, calculate a consequence and decide what a check establishes.

Unit 11 separates a field from a force and a potential from a potential energy. It also returns to a promise from Unit 6: when a force changes during a displacement, its work must be accumulated from small contributions. The integration developed in M101 now gives us a way to do that. Unit 12 will use charge and energy conservation to construct and test simple DC-circuit models. Unit 13 will bring light and electrical ideas together in atomic and spectral evidence, then consolidate the module’s approach to modelling.

The block contains 54 study hours, including ten hours of Python. The end-of-module assessment has its own separate 18-hour allocation. Unit 11 requires the force and energy ideas of P101 Units 4–6, the modelling judgement practised through Unit 10, and the definite-integral and antiderivative methods of M101 Units 8–9. Before the circuit work, you will also need the simultaneous-equation methods of M101 Unit 3. These are skill prerequisites; reaching a date in a timetable does not demonstrate them.

Our models will be deliberately bounded: fixed sources, simple fields, ideal circuit elements and elementary atomic descriptions. Each gives a useful foothold while leaving deeper questions for later physics. You will need paper, a calculator and your existing Python environment. No electrical apparatus or purchase is required.
