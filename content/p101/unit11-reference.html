Field and response		Fg=mg; Fe=qE.
[g]=Nkg−1; [E]=NC−1=Vm−1. Signed q.
Recall
Radial sources		Outward positive: gr=−GM/r2; Er=keQ/r2.
Point sources: r>0. Spherically symmetric exterior: r≥R>0; fixed sources.
Recall
Accumulation		W=∫abFx(x)dx [J]; Jx=∫tatbFnet,x(t)dt=Δpx [N s].
Start/end bounds; signed components; domain valid throughout.
Recall
Energy balance		ΔK=Wnet; K=12mv2.
Conservative field: Wfield=−ΔU; Δ(K+U)=Wother.
Recall
Potential		Ug=mϕ; ΔUe=qΔV.
[ϕ]=Jkg−1; [V]=JC−1 (volt). ΔV=VB−VA.
Recall
Point-source potential		Zero at infinity: ϕ=−GM/r; V=keQ/r.
Potentials add as scalars; fields as vectors. Exterior/point-source domains as above.
Understand
Work from endpoints		Wg=GMm(1/b−1/a).
We=keQq(1/a−1/b)=−qΔV.
Radial paths; fixed sources; same domain throughout.
Understand
Slope and reference		Ex=−dV/dx; gr=−dϕ/dr.
Uniform Ex: ΔV=−ExΔx.
Constant reference shifts leave fields and differences unchanged.
Understand
Magnetic response		Compass north end aligns with B [tesla, T].
Classical point charge: magnetic force is zero at rest or parallel motion; otherwise perpendicular to velocity and field. Magnetic force does no work on that particle.
