Motion and sustaining forces

UseTopicResult and conditions
RecallTiming and speedf=1/T; v=2πr/T=2πrf. Uniform circle: r radius (m), T period (s), f frequency (Hz), v speed (m/s). Lesson 1
RecallAngular measureθ=s/r in radians for arc length s. Uniform angular speed ω=2π/T (rad/s); v=rω and ar=rω2. A full turn is 2π radians. Lesson 1
UnderstandVector changeΔv=v2−v1; a―=Δv/Δt. Subtract vectors, not speeds. A finite average need not equal an instantaneous acceleration. Lesson 1
RecallRadial accelerationar=v2/r=4π2r/T2, inward. Velocity is tangent. For uniform speed, acceleration is entirely radial; if speed varies, a tangential component also exists. Lesson 1
UnderstandForce inventory∑Finward=mv2/r for the radial component of Newton’s second law. Draw real interactions only. “Centripetal” names the inward resultant, not an extra force. Lesson 2
ConsultFriction and supportFlat horizontal circle, weight/contact only vertically: N=mg. Sticking requires mv2/r≤μsN; hence vmax=μsgr. μs is a dimensionless friction coefficient. Lesson 2
ConsultConical stringString length L, angle θ to vertical: r=Lsinθ; Scosθ=mg; Ssinθ=mv2/r. Thus v2=rgtanθ for 0<θ<90∘. S is tension. Lesson 2
UnderstandVertical circleTop: S+mg=mvt2/r. Bottom: S−mg=mvb2/r. Inward positive at each. A cord needs S≥0; top condition vt≥gr is local, not a complete launch criterion. Lesson 2
UnderstandPhysical feasibilityAn unattached contact needs N≥0; a cord cannot push. Negative required support means the assumed constrained path fails. Do not take its absolute value. Lesson 2

Gravity and circular models

UseTopicResult and conditions
RecallGravity and fieldF=GMm/r2; g(r)=GM/r2=μ/r2; μ=GM. Attractive pair forces have equal magnitudes on different recipients. Exact for point masses or non-overlapping spherical mass distributions. Lesson 3
UnderstandDistance and unitsr=R+h: centre distance, source radius and altitude. G≈6.67430×10−11 N m²/kg²; μ in m³/s²; g in m/s² or N/kg. Weight mg differs from a support reading. Lesson 3
RecallCircular orbitv=μ/r; T=2πr3/μ. Exterior circular path, dominant spherical source treated as fixed, gravity only. Velocity must be tangential. Satellite mass cancels. Lesson 3
UnderstandControlled ratiosk=r/r0, q=μ/μ0: v/v0=q/k; T/T0=k3/q; g/g0=q/k2. Each point is a separate scenario, not time evolution. Lesson 4
