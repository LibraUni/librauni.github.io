## Dependencies and boundaries

M201 partial differentiation precedes analytical mechanics; vector calculus precedes relevant fields; Fourier/boundary methods precede their wave/quantum use. M102 supplies probability before P201 statistics. X201 propagates uncertainty after the needed derivatives, and uses P201 theory only once taught. Later project and option readiness depends on actual relevant outcomes; in particular P303 requires P302 foundations and cannot be assumed freely concurrent from day one. Calendars remain unresolved.

Intentional revisits increase mathematical depth and independence. Physical apparatus and collaboration remain unfulfilled outcomes without actual access. Optional telescope work is not designed or required.
