# M100 completion rubric review — 25 September 2026

Status: proposal awaiting learner review. Source curriculum/m100-rubric.js, public anchor #completion-rubric. No grades, enrolments, dates, submission system or awards changed.

## Immediate fit

The three approved completion principles become 18 observable indicators across E1–E6. Four evidence judgements distinguish absence of suitable evidence from a demonstrated gap and from successful performance. Proposed completion is criterion-based, without compensating weighted averages or an invented percentage pass threshold. Secure transfer is informative, not mandatory. Minor isolated slips are distinguished from errors that undermine the capability.

Existing TMA/EMA homes are retained. Explicit data interpretation, finite probability and debugging evidence prevent those skills disappearing inside a generic notebook. The task blueprint must sample every indicator across the assessment set, not every indicator in every task. Actual item difficulty and timing cannot be validated until tasks exist.

E6a/b uses the existing U12 hands-on exercise with final learner-controlled demonstration after guided practice. EMA C separately tests independent critique. A supplied trace cannot prove operation. Assistance conditions retain approved independent Python boundaries and add explicit task-level declarations for mathematics aids and taught scaffolding.

## Recovery and wider fit

Feedback identifies the indicator and actual evidence. Practice/corrections come before fresh equivalent reassessment of the affected indicators only. Preserve prior attempts and unaffected evidence; no proposed penalty or mark cap. Two unresolved focused reassessments trigger a teaching/prerequisite review, not exclusion. Planned feedback/revision stays within300h; additional support is recorded honestly and any deadline adjustment remains the learner’s choice.

Optional M100 remains outside degree credits. Full completion requires the complete evidence record and existing assessments; selected refreshers do not earn full-module credit. No new examination or grade implementation introduced. Privacy, backup and calendar semantics unchanged.
