## Reader review and revisions

A separate read-through checked the progression from quantity to unit conversion to scale, dimensions and estimation. Corrections/refinements made during review:

- Clarified that a shared unit does not establish shared endpoints or measurement procedure.
- Defined a rate’s unit before using a compound conversion; kept formal motion modelling in L02.
- Supplied circle/cylinder formulae in the estimate so geometry recall is not a hidden prerequisite.
- Distinguished negative exponents from negative values and stated the zero/negative-number notation cases.
- Explained why a dimension check cannot detect a cm-versus-m input error or establish numerical factors.
- Changed the rounded range wording to avoid implying that 0.7 L is a strict upper bound; included the π≈3.14 comparison and made the assumed nature of the range explicit.
- Offered experienced users a direct notebook check without forced reinstallation or busywork.

The explanations retain the same quantities across adjacent passages and return to the physical interpretation after arithmetic. Each activity uses already introduced methods; the final estimate is supported by a full worked example but adds explicit choice and critique. This is an author’s subject/reader review, not a separate human tutor review or student trial.
