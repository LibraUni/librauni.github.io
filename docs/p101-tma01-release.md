## Scope and independent-work design

Five original questions total 100 marks and retain the approved six hours: reading 15 minutes, questions 35/65/50/120/40, final checks 35. This is the separate B01 assessment allocation, not additional Unit 3 practice. Budget is an editorial estimate, not a learner timing trial. Actual time can be reported without concealing unfinished work. No force laws, calculus, fitting, statistical inference or uncertainty propagation enter the requirements. O1 is sampled only for motion and units; O5–O7 are sampled within taught limits. Supplied records do not demonstrate apparatus handling. Module pass thresholds and assessment weightings are not invented.

Questions ask for method selection, missing-evidence judgement, operational definitions, a controlled follow-up, an independent program, physical interpretation and a reproducible handover. The clock-origin comparison differs from the closing lesson’s comparison of two chosen velocities; the timing-method critique requires separating process variation from readout and bias. A blank notebook/script is deliberate independent evidence, not missing scaffolding. Consultation of teaching and documentation is permitted; generated assessed solutions are excluded and assistance must be declared. Marking credits valid alternative methods and carried-forward working. Private detailed solutions remain outside this repository; no reveal-answer control or solution download is published.
