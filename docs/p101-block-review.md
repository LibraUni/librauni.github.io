## Budget and purpose

B01 60h /20 included Python; B02 72h /10; B03 54h /8; B04 54h /10. Study240 plus orientation6, TMAs18, EMA18, revision12, feedback6 equals300. Python48 is included. Orientation covers setup and notebook workflow; B01 programming instruction is distinct. Practice/iCMAs are included. No assessment hours double-counted and no additional exam. This is an original estimate, not a verified OU weekly schedule.

The first block establishes measurement and computing, the second develops mechanics/conservation, the third waves/light, the fourth fields/circuits/atomic evidence and integration of established methods. All seven approved module outcomes have teaching and assessment homes. Three TMAs follow the first three blocks; O4 is checked formatively in B04 and explicitly in the EMA. The EMA includes both investigation and written breadth checks; task/component allocation remains pending.

## Prerequisites and capacity

B01 Python scalar/list/function teaching must precede M101 U02/U03 independent coding; arrays/plots must precede corresponding mathematical work. It can begin without calculus and need not await full B01 completion. M101 U01/U02 precede force resolution, U05 calculus-based motion, U08/U09 continuous work/impulse. Early B02 work uses constant-force/algebraic cases; the calculus application is reserved for B04. Unit-level pair reconciliation remains an open gate: block names alone do not establish timing feasibility.

B02/B04 are intentionally bounded and have capacity risks. Do not add a full thermodynamics, rotation or modern-physics survey. Elementary heat/internal-energy distinctions support O2; formal thermal/statistical physics requires a later core home. Circular motion does not establish angular-momentum or rigid-body coverage. Simple fields and atomic evidence do not establish full electromagnetic, quantum, solid-state or nuclear/particle coverage. Preserve these gaps in the degree register.

## Practical standard

Each block names an investigation, deliverable and validation. Free documented data/simulation routes remain available; actual measurement is encouraged where ordinary equipment and accessibility permit. Supplied-data analysis cannot establish apparatus handling; no laboratory/teamwork equivalence is claimed. Exact maintained free tools, licences, datasets and reproducibility checks belong to unit/release review. No required telescope, paid AI or optional M100 prerequisite.
