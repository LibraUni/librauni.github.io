Unit scope now has a teaching sequence, practice progression, physical application, independent evidence, misconceptions, planned visual explanations and a forward handover. These are unit-level specifications, not completed lesson plans. Scientific claims and numerical examples will require detailed checking when authored.

The six-hour TMA 01 now proposes an explicit 4.5/1/0.5-hour written/Python/submission split, with O6 added to its draft mapping. Feedback remains in week 10. Do not confuse unit-only hours with total presentation workload.

## Wider fit and forward handover

B01 begins E1/E2/E4 and rehearses communication/validation; it does not complete M100 exit evidence. B02 receives reliable quantities, expressions, domains and linear relations, and can then add graphical representations, functions and more structured Python. No functions/NumPy/plots are assessed before teaching. B03 later combines these foundations into modelling and controlled assistance. M101/P101 entry readiness benefits from this preparation; optional M100 never substitutes for degree outcomes.

At the next design step review B02 unit density, especially U08, and the cumulative Python sequence. Do not pre-write B02 lessons or revisit the fixed semester architecture.
