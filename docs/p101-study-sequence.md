# Studying P101 alongside M101

Local proposal · 28 September 2026 · No enrolment or dates set

Both modules retain their natural teaching order. Cross-module references offer help, not completion locks. Your existing coding and mathematics can supply a prerequisite; there is no need to repeat elementary work solely to synchronise the two modules. No competence or credit is recorded from this planning conversation.

## Opening sequence

Begin P101’s introduction and practical orientation alongside M101 Unit 1. P101 Unit 1 develops quantities → a constant-rate model → executable calculations → decisions/lists/loops → functions and checks → synthesis. M101 develops its vectors independently. Continue at a comfortable pace in each: a coding exercise need not wait for P101 if its methods are already familiar. Use the P101 lesson as a reference if needed.

Later connections are similarly light: M101 vector geometry supports P101 force resolution; its derivatives illuminate rates of motion; its integrals support accumulated work. P101 teaches the physical interpretation and assumptions. None of these links requires both module counters to match.

## Relative presentation proposal

A 35-week envelope is proposed for review, not an approved calendar or a timetable inferred from credits. The OU’s current [MST124 presentation](https://www.open.ac.uk/courses/modules/mst124/) lists 30 January–30 September 2027, approximately 35 weeks. That supports a long presentation reference for the mathematics strand. The public [SM123 description](https://www.open.ac.uk/courses/modules/sm123) was also inspected: it supports integrated physics/Python and concurrent mathematics, but supplies no current start-to-end presentation from which to verify a physics duration. Using the same envelope for P101 is our explicit local proposal. These are original unit windows, not a reproduction of an OU weekly schedule.

Each module retains 300 hours: 240 unit study + 6 orientation + 18 TMAs + 18 final assessment + 12 revision + 6 feedback. Across both, 600/35 is about 17.1 hours/week on average; actual weeks vary. M101’s written exam target is week 34 and P101’s EMA submission the end of week 35. The 18-hour M101 envelope includes preparation/checking/submission; it is not an 18-hour examination sitting. P101’s EMA retains 10 hours investigation, 6 written breadth and 2 instructions/checks/submission. Task-specific criteria, questions and exact exam sitting remain to be authored before assessment release.

Unit checkpoints/iCMAs remain inside the unit budgets, not added hours. Week 33 is deliberately a busier revision week (24 combined hours); week 34 combines the M101 assessment envelope with part of the P101 EMA. Staggered TMA deadlines reduce simultaneous submission pressure without flattening the workload. The final envelopes can be adjusted explicitly during calendar approval.
