# P101 opening production plan

28 September 2026 · Local v0.1 · First complete teaching increment

## Scope and budget

Module introduction and practical orientation: 6 hours outside the 240 unit-study hours. Allocate 30 minutes to the introduction/study method, 90 to environment preparation, 90 to notebook handling and recovery, 90 to a clean-run/reopen workflow and 60 to organisation/accessibility/troubleshooting. For an experienced user these are an envelope: use already familiar setup directly and spend appropriate time checking reproducibility; do not manufacture busywork or pretend unused hours were studied. Installation time varies and this estimate is not measured.

Unit 1 introduction and Lesson 1: 3 hours inside U01’s 18. Explanation/examples 75 min; written activity and reconstruction practice 75; feedback/correction 30; Python 0. Six later lessons are not implied: U01 contains six in total, of which only the first is authored here. The unit introduction is included in the first lesson’s explanation time.

Practice includes pausing to reconstruct worked examples before checking them, sketching/converting figures, seven numbered activities and revising answers. Timings are estimates to review against student experience, not a reading deadline. There is no separate lesson-end exercise set. U01-L06 reserves cumulative exercises, conclusion and unit web/PDF references.

## Prerequisites and representation

Assume signed arithmetic, fractions and elementary multiplication/division; retrieve powers of ten explicitly. No P101 lesson depends on a tick in M101. Use a few general dimensional statements without importing force, energy, calculus or statistical uncertainty teaching. Rate units are introduced as a quantity per elapsed time; the physical motion model belongs to L02. Generic SI reference entries are identified as lookup material, not assumed physics understanding.

Preserve the four original diagrams (one object/two scales; square units; scale ladder; cylinder) and the module's comparison table. Add NASA Earth–Moon observation imagery to the module introduction, NIST kilogram masses to the unit introduction, the NIST-F2 clock beside the SI context and a colourised leaf micrograph beside the scale discussion. Each receives an informative numbered caption, alt text and linked credit/reuse terms. The micrograph has no scale bar: explain the limitation rather than inventing lengths. Source register: docs/p101-media-manifest.json. Apply the programme-wide v1.6 before/during/after checks. Selective video and interactive provisions remain unchanged; notebook work begins in orientation and Lesson 2.

## Calibration commitments

Read the fixed M101 v0.4 sample, the published M101 introduction/Unit 1/Lesson 1 and actual private MST124 Unit 1 passages before drafting. Inspected MST124 pp.18, 73–76 and 96 cover units, powers, Activity 42, equation checking and mathematical communication; they do not constitute a physics-methods comparator. OpenStax University Physics 1 §§1.2–1.5 supplies a physics-level comparison, with its calculus and untaught mechanics excluded. Use BIPM to check current SI claims. Document original activity demand and all numerical/visual checks in the review. No private material is to appear in the preview or deliverables.
