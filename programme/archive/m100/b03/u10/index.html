U10-L01 · Vector components and direction

A vector has magnitude and direction; a scalar has magnitude without direction. Add and subtract vectors component by component. For (x,y), magnitude is √(x²+y²). A vector of magnitude r at angleθ has components (r cosθ,r sinθ). Unit vectors have magnitude1; divide a non-zero vector by its magnitude to obtain its direction unit vector. The zero vector has no unique direction. Use atan2(y,x), rather than arctan(y/x), to preserve quadrant information.

U10-L02 · Projections and numerical checks

The dot product a·b=aₓbₓ+aᵧbᵧ also equals |a||b|cosθ. It is a scalar. The signed projection of a onto a unit direction u is a·u; the vector projection is (a·u)u. NumPy arrays support component operations, np.dot computes the dot product and np.linalg.norm the magnitude. Check numerical results against a simple diagram and an exact calculation.
