U11-L01 · Summaries, variation and probability

The mean is sum divided by count; the median is the middle ordered value, averaging the two middle values for an even count. Range is maximum minus minimum. Population standard deviation is the square root of the average squared departure from the mean; the sample convention divides by n−1 instead. State which convention you use. Random variation creates scatter; a systematic offset can shift all readings together. Low scatter does not establish accuracy. A sample is not automatically representative of its population.

U11-L02 · A small data report without overclaiming

np.mean and np.median summarise an array. np.std defaults to population divisor n; ddof=1 selects n−1. A dot plot or histogram should identify units and sample size. Do not label observed SD as the uncertainty of a mean without further assumptions and analysis. Preserve data provenance: the values here are supplied synthetic observations for practice, not measurements you performed.
