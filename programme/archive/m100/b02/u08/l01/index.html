PROTOTYPE ARCHIVE

Geometry, radians and triangle methods

Similar triangles have equal corresponding angles and proportional sides. A right triangle satisfies a²+b²=c². Circle area is πr², circumference 2πr, and cylinder volume πr²h. A radian is arc length divided by radius: θ=s/r; 180°=π radians. On the unit circle cosθ is the horizontal coordinate and sinθ the vertical coordinate. Thus sin²θ+cos²θ=1 and tanθ=sinθ/cosθ where cosθ≠0. For other triangles, a/sinA=b/sinB and c²=a²+b²−2ab cosC; label opposite sides carefully.

Understand the relationship

Thus sin²θ+cos²θ=1 and tanθ=sinθ/cosθ where cosθ≠0. For other triangles, a/sinA=b/sinB and c²=a²+b²−2ab cosC; label opposite sides carefully.

Worked example

A 5 m ladder at 60° to the ground reaches 5sin60°=5√3/2≈4.33 m. An arc of length3 m at radius2 m spans1.5 rad. With a=5,b=8,A=30°, the sine rule gives sinB=0.8: B≈53.13° or126.87°, and both leave a positive third angle. The supplied information can describe two triangles.
