Periodic models and angle-unit checks

In y=A sin(ωt+φ)+c, |A| is amplitude, c the centre, and the period is 2π/|ω| when ω≠0. Python/NumPy trig uses radians. An inverse trig function returns a principal value; further angles can solve the same equation on a stated interval. For sinθ=1/2 on [0,2π), the solutions are π/6 and5π/6. Worked example

A 5 m ladder at 60° to the ground reaches 5sin60°=5√3/2≈4.33 m. An arc of length3 m at radius2 m spans1.5 rad. With a=5,b=8,A=30°, the sine rule gives sinB=0.8: B≈53.13° or126.87°, and both leave a positive third angle. The supplied information can describe two triangles.
