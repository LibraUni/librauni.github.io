U08-L01 · Geometry, radians and triangle methods

Similar triangles have equal corresponding angles and proportional sides. A right triangle satisfies a²+b²=c². Circle area is πr², circumference 2πr, and cylinder volume πr²h. A radian is arc length divided by radius: θ=s/r; 180°=π radians. On the unit circle cosθ is the horizontal coordinate and sinθ the vertical coordinate. Thus sin²θ+cos²θ=1 and tanθ=sinθ/cosθ where cosθ≠0. For other triangles, a/sinA=b/sinB and c²=a²+b²−2ab cosC; label opposite sides carefully.

U08-L02 · Periodic models and angle-unit checks

In y=A sin(ωt+φ)+c, |A| is amplitude, c the centre, and the period is 2π/|ω| when ω≠0. Python/NumPy trig uses radians. An inverse trig function returns a principal value; further angles can solve the same equation on a stated interval. For sinθ=1/2 on [0,2π), the solutions are π/6 and5π/6. Review a supplied erroneous suggestion only after solving independently: sin(60) is not sin60°.
