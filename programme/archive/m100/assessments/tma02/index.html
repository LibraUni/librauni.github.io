TMA 02

Numbers and algebra: explain, check and interpret

Archived prototype assessment · retained for historical reference. Submissions are closed.

Working independently

Notes, these lessons, a calculator and documentation for taught Python syntax are permitted. Write your own answers and code. Do not use generated solutions, agents or another person’s answers. Ask for clarification if wording is unclear; record any assistance that affects your work. Reading a page does not begin or submit an attempt.

Compact prototype assignment · U05–U07 · graphs, functions and quadratics · 6 hours · 100 marks. Use the existing planner deadline. Marking rewards explained method, accurate calculation, checks and interpretation; credit for valid follow-through is retained after a numerical slip.

Task 1

Written work (4 hours including preparation): (a) recover and check the line through (1,4),(4,13); discuss interpolation versus extrapolation (15 marks). (b) For f(x)=2x−5 and g(x)=x², compute both compositions at3, find the inverse of f and explain a domain restriction needed for an inverse of g (20 marks). (c) Solve x²−6x+5=0 by two methods and identify the vertex (20 marks). (d) Explain the domain and qualitative asymptotes of 1/(x−2), including why a plot must not connect across2 (15 marks).

Task 2

Notebook (2 hours including clean rerun and submission): write a scalar implementation of f, test three independently predicted values, and create labelled plots of the line and quadratic from the written questions. Explain one check and one limitation (30 marks). Submit a PDF and .ipynb.
