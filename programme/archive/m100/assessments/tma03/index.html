Working independently

Notes, these lessons, a calculator and documentation for taught Python syntax are permitted. Write your own answers and code. Do not use generated solutions, agents or another person’s answers. Ask for clarification if wording is unclear; record any assistance that affects your work. Reading a page does not begin or submit an attempt.

Compact prototype assignment · U08–U10 · geometry, exponential change and vectors · 6 hours · 100 marks. Use the existing planner deadline. Marking rewards explained method, accurate calculation, checks and interpretation; credit for valid follow-through is retained after a numerical slip.

Task 1

Written work (4 hours): (a) a right triangle has hypotenuse10 m and angle35° to its horizontal side. Find both components, checking Pythagoras; convert35° to radians (20 marks). (b) State amplitude and period of y=3sin(2πt), and solve sinθ=1/2 on [0,2π) (20 marks). (c) A supplied model N/N0=2^(−t/4) describes decay. Find the fraction at12 s and time to1/10; state assumptions (20 marks). (d) For a=(2,3), b=(−1,4), find sum, magnitudes and dot product; explain a horizontal projection (20 marks).

Task 2

Notebook and submission (2 hours): check your numerical trigonometry in radians, plot the supplied decay with labelled axes, and reproduce the vector calculations. Predict outputs first and explain a hand check. Submit PDF and .ipynb (20 marks).
