M100 · ASSESSMENT VERSION 1

EMA

Numbers and algebra: explain, check and interpret

Archived prototype assessment · retained for historical reference. Submissions are closed.

Working independently

Notes, these lessons, a calculator and documentation for taught Python syntax are permitted. Write your own answers and code. Do not use generated solutions, agents or another person’s answers. Ask for clarification if wording is unclear; record any assistance that affects your work. Reading a page does not begin or submit an attempt.

Compact prototype assignment · Whole-module independent synthesis · 18 hours · 100 marks. Use the existing planner deadline. Marking rewards explained method, accurate calculation, checks and interpretation; credit for valid follow-through is retained after a numerical slip.

Task 1

Component A — modelling notebook (10 hours, 50 marks). Use supplied synthetic times0,2,4,6 s and dimensionless intensity ratios1.00,0.71,0.49,0.36. Compare the supplied model I/I0=2^(−t/H) for H=3,4,5 s. Define the question, state domains/units/source, plot observations and predictions, compute residuals and compare sums of squared residuals. Choose the best of these three supplied candidates only; do not claim a fitted optimum or a confidence interval. Include functions, independent hand checks, a clean rerun and limitations. Marks: question/source10, transparent implementation15, checks10, interpretation15.

Task 2

Component B — handwritten mathematics (4 hours plus1 hour scanning/checking, 30 marks). Solve (x+2)/3+x/2=6 (5); solve x²−5x+4=0 and check roots (5); resolve magnitude8 at30° (5); solve2^(−t/4)=1/8 (5); find mean and range of2,3,7 (5); explain why observed scatter alone does not establish accuracy (5).

Task 3

Component C — critique and explanation (2 hours written plus1 hour tutor discussion, 20 marks). Independently critique this supplied suggestion: “Use exp(−t/H) instead; it is shorter and has the same half-life.” Give a counterexample and a correct exponential equivalent. Explain your model choice, tests and limits to the tutor. No live AI tool is needed. Submit the notebook and a PDF containing B and the written critique; arrange the discussion afterwards.
