B01 · Measure, model and compute

60 hours, including 20 Python hours. Prior blocks: Stage 1 entry readiness. Outcomes: P101-O1, P101-O5, P101-O6, P101-O7.

Purpose
Establish how a physical claim is constructed, measured and tested, while supplying the shared degree computing foundation.
Scope
Physical scales, SI units, dimensions and orders of magnitude; operational definitions, repeated measurements, instrument resolution, random and systematic effects, elementary descriptive statistics and justified precision. Position, displacement, elapsed time, average speed/velocity and graphs in one dimension. Compare an algebraic constant-rate model with observations; distinguish a model from its data.
Boundaries and mathematical prerequisites
No assumption of prior S111-style science study or optional M100 completion. No calculus-dependent motion, formal probability/inference or advanced uncertainty propagation. Statistical and apparatus depth must remain visible for later modules.
Python
Twenty included hours introduce variables, numeric types, conditions, loops, lists and functions, then small tables, basic arrays and labelled plots. Teach the scalar/list/function strand early enough for M101 U02/U03; array/plot teaching follows within B01 before M101 graph work. Six separate orientation hours cover installation, kernel choice and save/rerun workflow; do not charge these twice. Prepared learners demonstrate readiness and use equivalent-budget application practice.
Practical work and deliverable
A bounded repeated-measurement investigation with ordinary available objects/timing, or supplied documented measurements when equipment/access is unsuitable. Deliver a short notebook and a measurement table with units, provenance, a labelled plot and limitations. Supplied data cannot establish physical apparatus skill.
Evidence and assessment
Explain a measurement and its limitations, compute/interpret a rate, write a small program independently and reproduce its output after restart. TMA 01 follows B01 and samples the investigation, physical reasoning and coding; six hours in the separate TMA envelope.
Forward handover
P101-O6 supplies M101 code prerequisites in stages before B01 ends; no calculus is needed to teach them. P101-O5/O7 begin the practical entry contract for A101. Exact unit timing remains to be designed.

B02 · Motion, forces and conservation

72 hours, including 10 Python hours. Prior blocks: B01. Outcomes: P101-O1, P101-O2, P101-O5, P101-O6, P101-O7.

Purpose
Build a connected quantitative foundation through Newtonian models and conservation arguments.
Scope
Free-body diagrams, Newton’s laws, friction/tension and equilibrium; algebraic constant-acceleration motion and projectiles with supplied or derived algebraic relations. Uniform circular motion and Newtonian gravitation in simple planetary/satellite examples. Momentum, impulse in bounded cases, collisions, kinetic/potential energy and constant-force work. Distinguish heat, temperature, internal energy and work using simple energy balances; introduce an idealised microscopic picture with stated limits.
Boundaries and mathematical prerequisites
Components/projections require M101 U01/U02. Derivative-based velocity/acceleration waits for M101 U05; variable-force work and continuous impulse wait for U08/U09 and are revisited in B04. No hidden calculus-first entry, numerical ODE solver, rigid-body mechanics course, thermodynamic cycles or statistical mechanics.
Python
Use established functions/arrays to compare algebraic motion and conservation predictions with small supplied datasets. Track units and signs, inspect residual differences, check limiting cases and explain discrepancies; do not confuse simulation agreement with empirical evidence.
Practical work and deliverable
Compare a bounded motion dataset with two competing assumptions, or analyse a documented collision example. Submit reasoned diagrams and calculations with a short clean-runnable notebook; retain hand-computable checks.
Evidence and assessment
Select a system, distinguish interactions from motion, justify a balance and challenge an implausible answer. TMA 02 follows B02 and samples mechanics, energy/heat distinctions, measurement critique and independently explained code; six separate hours.
Forward handover
Supports later P201 mechanics/thermal development and A101 gravity/scale interpretation. Angular momentum and extended-body rotation require explicit later core allocation; a circular-orbit example does not cover them.

B03 · Oscillations, waves and light

54 hours, including 8 Python hours. Prior blocks: B02. Outcomes: P101-O3, P101-O5, P101-O6, P101-O7.

Purpose
Connect periodic models with observable signals and the information carried by light.
Scope
Amplitude, phase, period and frequency from graphs and simple trigonometric models; supplied simple-harmonic-motion solutions and their physical limits. Wave speed, wavelength, superposition and standing-wave patterns; qualitative resonance/damping, interference and diffraction. Reflection/refraction and elementary image or ray geometry; electromagnetic spectrum, intensity and inverse-square spreading with assumptions.
Boundaries and mathematical prerequisites
No complex amplitudes, Fourier analysis, wave-equation solution or ODE derivation of oscillators before M102. Quantitative diffraction theory and advanced optics remain later. Sampling is introduced operationally, not as a complete signal-processing course.
Python
Generate and sample a sinusoidal model, vary sampling and compare with a documented small signal. Make labelled plots, recover a period by transparent inspection/calculation and show a misleading sampling case; avoid unexplained fitting or spectral libraries.
Practical work and deliverable
A reproducible signal investigation using supplied free data or an accessible simulation; compare predictions with a known benchmark and report what the sampling permits. No telescope or paid service required.
Evidence and assessment
Relate an observed pattern to a wave model, calculate a propagation quantity and explain an optical/sampling limitation. TMA 03 follows B03, sampling O3/O5/O6/O7 with declared earlier concepts; six separate hours.
Forward handover
Provides A101 light, intensity and signal-readiness foundations. Spectral shifts, detector calibration and astronomical inference remain A101 responsibilities. M102 later derives oscillator solutions.

B04 · Fields, circuits and microscopic evidence

54 hours, including 10 Python hours. Prior blocks: B01, B02, B03. Outcomes: P101-O1, P101-O2, P101-O3, P101-O4, P101-O5, P101-O6, P101-O7.

Purpose
Extend model-based reasoning across field, electrical and atomic scales, then consolidate investigative judgement.
Scope
Field versus force and potential versus potential energy; simple gravitational/electric examples, charge and qualitative magnetic interactions. Current, voltage, resistance, series/parallel DC circuits, power and conservation with small algebraic systems. Atomic structure, discrete energy levels and photon/spectral evidence; microscopic pictures of conduction/material behaviour with clear limits. Revisit a variable-force work or impulse model after M101 integration and connect hand, data and code checks.
Boundaries and mathematical prerequisites
No Gauss/Maxwell formalism, AC impedance, circuit ODEs, quantitative quantum mechanics, solid-state band theory or nuclear/particle coverage claim. Circuit algebra uses M101 U03; calculus revisit requires U08/U09. If the pair timetable cannot supply those in time, revise the sequence openly before release.
Python
Compare a small circuit/field calculation with a hand benchmark; inspect a supplied spectrum or model output without duplicating A101 calibration. Prepare a short reproducible investigation using established methods, including edge-case checks and transparent assistance disclosure.
Practical work and deliverable
A bounded virtual DC-circuit or equivalent documented-data investigation with explicit model parameters and measurement limits. Free accessible tools and dataset licensing must be checked during unit design. Simulation competence does not establish wiring or laboratory safety skills.
Evidence and assessment
Embedded formative checks prepare an EMA with two clearly identified components: a bounded investigation and written conceptual/quantitative checks across O1–O7. The 18-hour EMA envelope includes final assessment preparation/submission; teaching practice here is distinct. No additional exam or fourth TMA.
Forward handover
A101 receives measurement, light, notebook and evidence skills; X201 develops investigation/inference and actual apparatus experience. Formal field, quantum, thermal and matter foundations remain compulsory later core work.

P101 / COHERENCE REVIEW

A concurrent pair, not two disconnected modules.

The block-construction gate in policy 1.0 is met by the approved Stage 1 peer map and provisional wider degree progression. M101’s approved units specify the mathematical handovers. Construction disposition: block proposal permitted. Release disposition: no teaching, timetable or enrolment.

RequirementDesign response
