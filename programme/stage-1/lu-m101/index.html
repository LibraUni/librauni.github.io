B01 · Vectors and linear relationships

54 study hours, including 4 Python hours · Prior blocks: Stage 1 entry readiness · Outcomes: M101-O3, M101-O4, M101-O6

Purpose
Express direction and simultaneous relationships precisely, providing mathematical tools for early P101 mechanics.
Scope
Coordinate vectors in two and three dimensions; magnitude, components, addition and scalar multiplication; scalar products, projections and elementary vector products with geometric meaning. Matrices as transformations; matrix-vector multiplication, composition and small linear systems by elimination. Recognise inconsistent and underdetermined systems; interpret elementary determinants and invertibility in small examples.
Boundaries
Retrieve algebra and trigonometry within the block, not a second full bridge. No abstract vector-space axioms, general eigenproblem or multivariable calculus. M102 extends bases, orthogonality and eigenvectors. P101 teaches the physics of forces; mathematical applications here supply required physical assumptions.
Python
After P101 scalar/list/function induction, check a small vector or matrix calculation against hand work using readable code. No unexplained numerical solver. If the computing handover is late, written teaching can begin but independent code assessment waits; no extra M101 installation induction.
Expected evidence
Resolve a vector, explain a product geometrically, apply a transformation and solve/classify a small linear system. Explain a code check and its limits.
Forward handover
Vector components and projections must reach P101 before force-resolution tasks. Matrix methods prepare M102 linear algebra. Full block completion is not required before the first vector handover.
Assessment role
TMA 01 follows this block: vectors, transformations, small systems, mathematical explanation and an independently written short check. Six hours remain in the separate TMA envelope.

B02 · Change and local behaviour

72 study hours, including 8 Python hours · Prior blocks: B01 · Outcomes: M101-O1, M101-O5, M101-O6

Purpose
Develop derivatives from rates and limits, then use them to reason about the behaviour of functions.
Scope
Functions and domains as targeted retrieval; limits and continuity through examples and counterexamples; derivative from difference quotients. Elementary power, exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric derivatives; product, quotient and chain rules; implicit differentiation and second derivatives. Curve behaviour, extrema, related rates and tangent-line approximation. State hypotheses when invoking a theorem; introduce the mean-value idea without a full real-analysis course.
Boundaries
No general epsilon-delta analysis course, infinite Taylor-series theory or ODE solution catalogue. Linear approximation is taught here; systematic series belong to M102. Keep substantial abstract practice alongside supplied physical rate models.
Python
Compare analytical derivatives with forward/central finite differences at ordinary and problematic points; vary step size and notice truncation versus round-off. Plot only after P101 array/plot handover. Python functions represent mathematics, not proof.
Expected evidence
Derive a simple derivative from its definition, select rules correctly, justify an extremum and explain when a derivative/linear approximation is unsuitable. Check a numerical result against analytical reasoning.
Forward handover
M101-O1 reaches P101 before calculus-based velocity/acceleration problems. Tangent approximation and derivative checks support M102 and later data/model investigations.
Assessment role
TMA 02 follows this block: differentiation, interpretation, local approximation and an explained independent numerical check. Six hours outside block study.

B03 · Accumulation and integral models

78 study hours, including 10 Python hours · Prior blocks: B02 · Outcomes: M101-O2, M101-O5, M101-O6

Purpose
Connect local rates with accumulated quantities and develop reliable analytical and numerical integration.
Scope
Definite integrals as limits of sums and signed accumulation; indefinite integrals and constants; fundamental theorem of calculus. Elementary antiderivatives, substitution and introductory integration by parts. Area, average values and simple volumes; supplied work and accumulated-flow models with dimensional interpretation. Rectangle/midpoint/trapezoidal estimates and refinement checks; explain the difference between an approximation and a justified error bound.
Boundaries
Further rational/trigonometric integration techniques, improper integrals and systematic series move to M102 for explicit allocation. Multiple and line/surface integrals remain M201. A signed integral need not equal geometrical area or distance; split the domain when needed. This is not a duplicate numerical-methods course.
Python
Construct a transparent quadrature calculation, compare with an analytic integral and a hand-computable subset, vary resolution and diagnose a misleading apparently converged value. Validate units and interval orientation.
Expected evidence
Explain an integral as accumulation, use the fundamental theorem with appropriate conditions, select a taught technique and connect signs/units to the result. Produce and critique an independent numerical integration check.
Forward handover
Integral accumulation and scalar-product geometry precede the corresponding P101 work examples. Integration foundations prepare M102 probability/ODEs and A101 interpretation of sampled signals.
Assessment role
TMA 03 follows this block: analytical integration, interpretation and independent quadrature work; cumulative earlier mathematics may be sampled. Six hours outside block study.

B04 · Approximation, validation and mathematical judgement

36 study hours, including 8 Python hours · Prior blocks: B01, B02, B03 · Outcomes: M101-O1, M101-O2, M101-O3, M101-O4, M101-O5, M101-O6

Purpose
Connect the module’s tools and decide when an answer or approximation deserves confidence.
Scope
Synthesis with a bounded supplied physical model: choose representations, identify domains/scales, compare local linear and accumulated predictions, vary a numerical resolution, and investigate sensitivity to inputs. Separate model assumptions, approximation error and arithmetic/computing mistakes. Explain agreement or disagreement between hand, graphical and computational results. Local Newton iteration may illustrate a derivative application after its assumptions and failure cases are introduced; no general numerical-analysis theory is implied.
Boundaries
This is taught mathematical validation and transfer, not the separately budgeted revision period or an extra EMA. Reuse methods from B01–B03 and deepen error reasoning; do not smuggle in complex numbers, eigenmethods, ODE solvers or Taylor-series theory. No required AI/agent operation.
Python
Combine established calculations into a short clean-runnable notebook, identify an edge case and repair a supplied flawed check. Optional assistance must be declared and does not substitute for independent TMA evidence.
Expected evidence
Formative synthesis and feedback: explain method choice, provide independent checks, challenge a plausible wrong result and communicate limitations. Final written exam samples transferable reasoning; executed-code evidence comes from the TMAs.
Forward handover
Prepare M102 and A101 to expect explained code, analytical checks, explicit assumptions and justified approximation. Do not declare those capabilities attained from participation alone.
Assessment role
Embedded formative checkpoint plus later final examination. No fourth TMA or additional assessment hours. Core coding capabilities have already been sampled independently in TMAs; practical extensions here remain formative.

B01 / U01 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Coordinates, vectors and components

18 study hours, including 0 Python hours. Prior units: Module entry readiness. Outcome mapping: M101-O3.

Purpose
Represent magnitude and direction consistently.
Scope and learning targets
Coordinates in two and three dimensions; displacement vectors, magnitude, unit vectors, addition and scalar multiplication; resolve components using trigonometry. Retrieve algebra, angles and units through short entry checks and abstract practice.
Boundary
No force laws, abstract vector spaces or calculus. Longer readiness remediation is separately scoped.
Python and external prerequisites
Hand and diagram work; no coding prerequisite.
Evidence and assessment
Resolve and reconstruct vectors, distinguish position from displacement and explain signs and units. Practice contributes to TMA 01 readiness.
Forward handover
P101 may use components after this unit, before the rest of B01 is complete.

B01 / U02 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Products, projections and orientation

16 study hours, including 1 Python hours. Prior units: U01. Outcome mapping: M101-O3, M101-O6.

Purpose
Relate vector calculations to angles and geometry.
Scope and learning targets
Scalar product, angle and orthogonality; scalar/vector projections; elementary cross product, magnitude, orientation and parallel cases. Distinguish scalar and vector outputs; supplied work/torque contexts explain their assumptions.
Boundary
Vector calculus and physical derivations of work or torque are outside scope.
Python and external prerequisites
Requires P101 scalar arithmetic, lists and functions first. Check a projection or cross product using transparent component calculations.
Evidence and assessment
Explain a projection geometrically, check perpendicularity and right-hand orientation; compare hand/code results, including a degenerate case. TMA 01 evidence.
Forward handover
P101 projections before force resolution; product geometry before later work/torque applications.

B01 / U03 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Matrices, transformations and simultaneous equations

20 study hours, including 3 Python hours. Prior units: U01, U02. Outcome mapping: M101-O4, M101-O6.

Purpose
Represent and classify small coupled linear relationships.
Scope and learning targets
Matrix-vector multiplication and composition in order; two-dimensional transformations; elimination in small systems, including a guided three-unknown example. Unique, inconsistent and dependent systems; elementary determinants and invertibility in two dimensions.
Boundary
No general determinant expansion syllabus, abstract vector-space theory or eigenproblems; those need M102 allocation.
Python and external prerequisites
P101 list/function foundation required. Code a small transformation and verify a hand-solved system by substitution, without a black-box solver.
Evidence and assessment
Solve and classify systems, explain composition order and interpret a zero determinant. Embedded iCMA 01 then TMA 01 samples U01–U03.
Forward handover
M102 extends bases, orthogonality and eigenmethods; P101 need not wait for this unit to use vectors.

B02 / U04 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Functions, limits and continuity

16 study hours, including 1 Python hours. Prior units: U03. Outcome mapping: M101-O1, M101-O5, M101-O6.

Purpose
Establish when local reasoning about a function is justified.
Scope and learning targets
Targeted retrieval of domains, composition and elementary functions; finite and one-sided limits, algebraic simplification and continuity; examples where a function value and limiting value differ. Numerical evidence and its limitations.
Boundary
No full epsilon-delta development or infinite-series theory. Arrays/plots are only used after their P101 handover.
Python and external prerequisites
Evaluate a sequence of nearby inputs with scalar functions; compare numerical suggestions with algebraic reasoning.
Evidence and assessment
Determine a limit, state a domain restriction and give a continuity counterexample; explain why a table is not a proof. TMA 02 preparation.
Forward handover
Limits support derivative and integral definitions; discontinuities reappear in U11 validation.

B02 / U05 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Derivatives and differentiation methods

24 study hours, including 2 Python hours. Prior units: U04. Outcome mapping: M101-O1, M101-O6.

Purpose
Connect a local rate to a precise mathematical operation.
Scope and learning targets
Difference quotient and derivative definition; a simple first-principles derivation; power, exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric derivatives (radians). Product, quotient and chain rules; implicit differentiation and second derivatives. Plenty of abstract fluency practice.
Boundary
No ODE solution methods or multivariable differentiation.
Python and external prerequisites
After P101 functions, compare a hand derivative with nearby secant slopes. Plotting is conditional on P101 array/plot induction.
Evidence and assessment
Derive a simple derivative, explain each rule choice, distinguish derivative value from function value and check units. TMA 02 evidence.
Forward handover
P101 calculus-based velocity and acceleration follow this unit; earlier mechanics can use supplied constant-rate models.

B02 / U06 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Rates, extrema and the shape of a function

18 study hours, including 2 Python hours. Prior units: U05. Outcome mapping: M101-O1, M101-O5, M101-O6.

Purpose
Use derivatives to make justified claims about behaviour.
Scope and learning targets
Monotonicity, stationary points, second derivative and concavity; extrema on a closed interval including endpoints. Related rates in supplied models; mean-value theorem statement, hypotheses and geometric meaning. Counterexamples to careless inference.
Boundary
No general optimisation algorithms or proof-based real analysis.
Python and external prerequisites
After P101 plotting induction, compare analytical features with a graph and deliberately vary the plotting window or sampling.
Evidence and assessment
Justify an extremum and its domain, formulate a related-rate relation and diagnose a misleading graph. TMA 02 evidence.
Forward handover
P101 rate interpretation and M102 model analysis; calculus claims retain their hypotheses.

B02 / U07 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Linear approximation and numerical derivatives

14 study hours, including 3 Python hours. Prior units: U05, U06. Outcome mapping: M101-O1, M101-O5, M101-O6.

Purpose
Choose and test a local approximation.
Scope and learning targets
Tangent-line approximation, locality and units; forward and central finite differences; step-size experiments distinguishing truncation and floating-point cancellation. Compare against a known derivative; contrast empirical error with a proved bound.
Boundary
Systematic Taylor expansions and advanced error analysis belong later; sensitivity is not a full uncertainty course.
Python and external prerequisites
Write finite-difference functions, test ordinary and problematic points, tabulate step size and error; plotting only after P101 induction.
Evidence and assessment
Explain where linearisation fails and why reducing step size need not improve a numerical derivative. Embedded iCMA 02 then TMA 02 samples U04–U07.
Forward handover
M102 series extends the local idea; A101 expects approximation checks, not blind numerical trust.

B03 / U08 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Accumulation and the fundamental theorem

20 study hours, including 1 Python hours. Prior units: U07. Outcome mapping: M101-O2, M101-O6.

Purpose
Connect a rate with the quantity accumulated over an interval.
Scope and learning targets
Finite sums leading to definite integrals; signed accumulation, interval orientation, antiderivatives and constants. Both directions of the fundamental theorem with appropriate continuity conditions; elementary antiderivatives and initial values.
Boundary
No differential-equation solution catalogue, improper integrals or multiple integration.
Python and external prerequisites
Construct a small rectangle sum with existing loops/functions and compare with an exactly integrable case.
Evidence and assessment
Explain the rate/accumulation connection, differentiate an accumulation function and evaluate an integral with units. TMA 03 preparation.
Forward handover
P101 integral-based work/flow follows U08 plus U02; M102 ODE/probability work relies on this foundation.

B03 / U09 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Choosing an integration method

24 study hours, including 2 Python hours. Prior units: U08. Outcome mapping: M101-O2, M101-O6.

Purpose
Select and verify elementary antiderivatives.
Scope and learning targets
Elementary integral fluency, substitution with transformed limits and introductory integration by parts. Recognise structure and check an antiderivative by differentiating; distinguish definite and indefinite answers.
Boundary
Further rational/trigonometric techniques, improper integrals and systematic series are deferred explicitly to M102.
Python and external prerequisites
Evaluate selected results at sample endpoints; numerical agreement supplements a derivative check and does not establish an identity.
Evidence and assessment
Explain method choice, preserve constants/limits and reject an incorrect antiderivative. TMA 03 evidence.
Forward handover
M102 extends techniques; P101 applications use only techniques already available.

B03 / U10 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Integral models, signs and dimensions

18 study hours, including 2 Python hours. Prior units: U08, U09, U02. Outcome mapping: M101-O2, M101-O6.

Purpose
Translate a bounded physical or geometric question into an integral.
Scope and learning targets
Area versus signed area, displacement versus distance with reversal, mean value of a function and simple volumes by slices. Supplied work and accumulated-flow models; identify integrand, bounds, dimensions and assumptions.
Boundary
No new physical laws assumed; no surface/line integrals or general solids techniques.
Python and external prerequisites
Use existing functions and plots to inspect domains, sign changes and plausible scale before calculation.
Evidence and assessment
Formulate and explain an integral, split an interval where required and check dimensions and limiting cases. TMA 03 evidence.
Forward handover
P101 owns the physical theory; A101 later interprets accumulated signals using these distinctions.

B03 / U11 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Numerical integration and trustworthy checks

16 study hours, including 5 Python hours. Prior units: U09, U10. Outcome mapping: M101-O2, M101-O5, M101-O6.

Purpose
Compute an approximation and test whether it is credible.
Scope and learning targets
Rectangle, midpoint and trapezoidal quadrature; resolution refinement and analytical benchmarks. Smooth versus problematic functions, interval orientation, missed features and misleading convergence; distinguish an estimate from a justified bound.
Boundary
No adaptive-library methods, Simpson-rule syllabus or formal numerical-analysis course required.
Python and external prerequisites
P101 arrays/plotting and prior functions required. Implement transparent quadrature, check a hand-computable subset and a known integral, test an edge case and explain refinement results.
Evidence and assessment
Independent readable code with numerical/analytical comparisons, an identified failure mode and limitations. Embedded iCMA 03 then TMA 03 samples U08–U11 and stated cumulative items.
Forward handover
A101 numerical signal work; later computational modules deepen convergence and error analysis.

B04 / U12 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Validation, sensitivity and competing answers

18 study hours, including 4 Python hours. Prior units: U03, U07, U11. Outcome mapping: M101-O1, M101-O2, M101-O4, M101-O5, M101-O6.

Purpose
Distinguish disagreement caused by assumptions, approximations or code.
Scope and learning targets
Within a supplied bounded model, compare hand, graphical and computational answers; vary an input or resolution, identify domain/scale restrictions and separate model, approximation and implementation error. Critique and repair a supplied flawed check.
Boundary
Reuse established methods; no new ODE, eigenvalue or series syllabus. Newton iteration is an optional substitution for an example within these hours, not extra required work.
Python and external prerequisites
Clean-runnable notebook, independent benchmark and edge case; explain any assistance. No required AI agent use.
Evidence and assessment
Formative feedback on justified checks, sensitivity and error diagnosis; written reasoning informs final exam preparation. No extra TMA.
Forward handover
M102/A101 receive expectations for reproducibility and explicit model limitations.

B04 / U13 · APPROVED UNIT BLUEPRINT

Connecting mathematical representations

18 study hours, including 4 Python hours. Prior units: U12. Outcome mapping: M101-O1, M101-O2, M101-O3, M101-O4, M101-O5, M101-O6.

Purpose
Transfer the module’s tools to a coherent unfamiliar setting.
Scope and learning targets
A scaffolded synthesis using a supplied planar motion or transport model with explicit assumptions: vector/matrix representation, local rate and accumulated quantity. Choose methods, compare representations and explain approximation limits; short transfer questions vary the setting.
Boundary
Taught synthesis, not the separate 12-hour revision allocation, an EMA or a demand to discover new physics. Assess independence through existing TMAs, not participation.
Python and external prerequisites
Combine known functions into a reproducible short investigation, include an analytical and edge-case check and communicate limitations.
Evidence and assessment
Formative synthesis checkpoint covers all six outcomes. Final written examination samples reasoning and supplied-code interpretation; executed coding evidence remains in TMAs.
Forward handover
Ready for M102/A101 only through demonstrated capabilities; this outline does not establish learner attainment.
